Tank Lab

Drive a 30-tonne tank around a 5 × 5 km world. Destroy the red & white targets scattered across the map — six of them sit on the firing range just north of the training ground.

  • WS / ↑↓ Drive / reverse
  • AD / ←→ Skid-steer
  • Shift Brake (lock tracks)
  • Mouse Aim turret · QE rotate turret
  • Click / Space / F Fire
  • Wheel / +− Zoom
  • Z Camera dead zone · X Speed zoom
  • M Full map · N Sound
  • R Back to start · P Pause
  • H This help

Shells knock props around, set barrels off, fell trees and bring down buildings. Ram crates and trees at speed to flatten them. Roads are fast, fields and sand are slow, and hills push back.

The handling is built on a classic 2D rigid-body vehicle model: each track contact point resists sliding sideways much more than rolling forwards, and the tank turns by driving its two tracks at different speeds.
